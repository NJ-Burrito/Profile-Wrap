# Hook formulas

Twenty hook structures from `data/hook-formulas.json` (the file is the source; this page is generated from it).
A formula is a shape for the first line, not text to paste. Pick by the persona's goal and the
engagement you want, offer three, recommend one. Examples are fictional and carry no figures; any number
you add must come through `meta.claims`.

| Id | Name | Good for | Aims at | Never for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `anaphora` | Anaphora | authority, network | saves | a topic with only one point |
| `rip` | R.I.P. the old way | authority, clients | comments | sensitive topics; when the old way is the reader's employer |
| `year_pivot` | Year pivot | authority, hiring | saves | when no real change happened |
| `time_anchor` | Time anchor | authority, network | comments | stories you cannot source |
| `curiosity_gap` | Curiosity gap | authority, clients | saves | when the payoff is weak; the reader feels cheated |
| `contrarian` | Contrarian | authority | comments | no evidence to show; attacking a named person |
| `false_binary` | False binary | authority | comments | when the two options really are exclusive |
| `emotional_cold_open` | Emotional cold open | network, authority | likes | private or third-party grief |
| `named_gratitude` | Named gratitude | network | likes | without the person's consent; private_terms people |

## Structures and examples

### Anaphora (`anaphora`)

- Structure: Repeat one opening phrase three times, then land the point.
- Arabic: كرّر جملة افتتاحية ثلاث مرات ثم اذكر الخلاصة.
- Example: I stopped guessing. I stopped copying. I started measuring.

### R.I.P. the old way (`rip`)

- Structure: R.I.P. {old habit}. Here is what replaced it.
- Arabic: وداعا لـ {عادة قديمة}. وهذا ما حلّ مكانها.
- Example: R.I.P. the monthly status report.

### Year pivot (`year_pivot`)

- Structure: In {year} I did X. In {year} I do Y. The gap is the lesson.
- Arabic: في {سنة} كنت أفعل س. اليوم أفعل ص. الفرق هو الدرس.
- Example: In 2019 I handed in reports. Now I hand in decisions.

### Time anchor (`time_anchor`)

- Structure: {When} I {did X}. Here is what happened next.
- Arabic: {متى} فعلت س. وهذا ما جرى بعد ذلك.
- Example: On Monday I deleted a dashboard nobody opened.

### Curiosity gap (`curiosity_gap`)

- Structure: State the result, hold back the method until line three.
- Arabic: اذكر النتيجة وأجّل الطريقة إلى السطر الثالث.
- Example: One chart fixed the weekly argument.

### Contrarian (`contrarian`)

- Structure: Common belief X is wrong for {audience}. Here is the evidence.
- Arabic: الاعتقاد الشائع س خاطئ لـ {الجمهور}. وهذه الأدلة.
- Example: More data rarely settles the argument.

### False binary (`false_binary`)

- Structure: Skip the A-or-B debate; the order you do them in matters more.
- Arabic: تجاوز جدال أ أو ب؛ ترتيب التنفيذ أهم.
- Example: Skip the tool debate. The order of the questions matters more.

### Emotional cold open (`emotional_cold_open`)

- Structure: Open on the moment, one sentence, no context.
- Arabic: ابدأ باللحظة نفسها في جملة واحدة بلا تمهيد.
- Example: The room went quiet when the chart loaded.

### Named gratitude (`named_gratitude`)

- Structure: Thank a real person for one specific thing they did.
- Arabic: اشكر شخصا حقيقيا على شيء محدد فعله.
- Example: Thanks to a teammate who read my draft twice.

### Numbered list (`numbered_list`)

- Structure: {N} things about {topic}, one line each.
- Arabic: {عدد} أشياء عن {الموضوع} في سطر لكل منها.
- Example: Five checks before I send a report.

### Before and after (`before_after`)

- Structure: Before: X. After: Y. The change: Z.
- Arabic: قبل: س. بعد: ص. ما تغيّر: ع.
- Example: Before: ten tabs of figures. After: one page.

### Mistake confession (`mistake_confession`)

- Structure: I got X wrong. Here is what it cost and what I do now.
- Arabic: أخطأت في س. وهذه كلفته وما أفعله الآن.
- Example: I shipped a chart with the axis cut off.

### Behind the scenes (`behind_the_scenes`)

- Structure: Show the unglamorous middle of a piece of work.
- Arabic: أظهر الجزء غير اللامع من منتصف العمل.
- Example: What a messy spreadsheet looks like before it becomes a chart.

### Question with stakes (`question_stakes`)

- Structure: Ask one concrete question and say why the answer matters.
- Arabic: اطرح سؤالا محددا واشرح لماذا تهمّ الإجابة.
- Example: Which report did your team stop reading, and why?

### Myth and fact (`myth_fact`)

- Structure: Myth: X. Fact: Y, with the source.
- Arabic: خرافة: س. حقيقة: ص، مع المصدر.
- Example: Myth: a dashboard is a decision. Fact: a dashboard is a question.

### One-line lesson (`one_line_lesson`)

- Structure: Lead with the lesson, then the short story that earned it.
- Arabic: ابدأ بالدرس ثم القصة القصيرة التي أنتجته.
- Example: Write the decision first, then the analysis.

### Reframe a quote (`reframe_quote`)

- Structure: Quote a familiar line and change one word.
- Arabic: اقتبس عبارة مألوفة وغيّر كلمة واحدة.
- Example: Done is better than perfect; reviewed is better than done.

### Mini case (`mini_case`)

- Structure: Problem, move, result in four short lines.
- Arabic: مشكلة، خطوة، نتيجة في أربعة أسطر قصيرة.
- Example: A clinic could not see its no-show pattern. I plotted it. They moved the reminder.

### Checklist promise (`checklist_promise`)

- Structure: Promise a checklist in the hook and deliver it in full.
- Arabic: عِد بقائمة تحقق في الافتتاحية وسلّمها كاملة.
- Example: A checklist I run before I share any chart.

### Availability note (`availability_note`)

- Structure: Say what you do, for whom, and when you have room.
- Arabic: اذكر ما تفعله ولمن ومتى يتوفر لديك وقت.
- Example: I help small non-profits read their own data. I have room for one project in spring.

## Choosing

- `authority`: contrarian, myth_fact, year_pivot, one_line_lesson, mistake_confession.
- `clients`: mini_case, before_after, checklist_promise, availability_note.
- `hiring`: year_pivot, mini_case, availability_note (advice only; no open-to-work wording unless the goal is `job_seeking`).
- `network`: named_gratitude, question_stakes, emotional_cold_open, time_anchor.

Rules for every hook: under `gate.hook_max` characters, no greeting opener, no contrast shape
("it's not X, it's Y"), no engagement bait, no unverified figure.
