Profile-Wrap

Your LinkedIn, on autopilot. You stay the boss.

Profile-Wrap is a free, open-source LinkedIn post manager. It plans your week of posts, writes them in your voice and checks every one before it goes out.

Download the latest release Source on GitHub PyPI package

What it does for you

  • Plans your week of posts
  • Writes them in your voice
  • Checks every post before it goes out
  • Works in English and Arabic

Four ways to use it

Pick one. All are free.

  • Plugin
    For Claude Code and Codex.
  • Skills
    For any AI agent that reads skills.
  • MCP server
    For any app that speaks MCP.
  • Desktop app
    No AI app? Use this. Windows, Mac and Linux.

Install steps are in the README.

You tap Allow

  • Every post waits on your board. Nothing goes out unless you tap Allow.
  • It uses LinkedIn's own official way in, and it never asks for your LinkedIn password.
  • It never reads your private messages.

Free and open source

Profile-Wrap is free under the MIT licence. Writing uses your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini account, or a free local one. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
