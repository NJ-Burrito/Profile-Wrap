# Planning method

A plan is useful when someone can act on it on Monday. This is the order to think in.

## 1. Start from the reader, not the calendar

Write one sentence: "The person I want to reach is ___, and after reading, they should ___." That is
`persona.audience` and `persona.goal`. Everything below serves that sentence; an idea that serves a different
one waits.

Goals change the plan:

- `authority`: depth on a few topics, repeated from different angles; fewer formats, more consistency.
- `clients`: problems the client has, shown through finished work; one clear next step in the first comment.
- `job_seeking`: proof of skill in public; advice only, and no "open to work" wording in posts.
- `hiring`: what the team does and how it works; real details beat slogans.
- `network`: questions and replies; more engagement work, fewer broadcasts.

## 2. Pillars

Three to four pillars, each with a share that sums to about 1. A pillar is a theme the persona can speak on
with facts, not a topic of the week. Check each pillar against the facts register: a pillar with no verified
facts behind it can only produce opinion posts, and those need the persona's own experience from the story
bank.

Rotate by deficit: the pillar furthest below its share goes next. `profile-wrap calendar fill` does this when it adds
open slots.

## 3. Formats

Choose the format from the idea, not from habit:

| Idea shape | Format |
|---|---|
| One insight, one sentence | text |
| A list or a sequence of steps | slides (one point per slide) |
| A mechanism, before and after, something that moves | GIF |

Take the evidence in `docs/references/growth-evidence.md` as the starting point and label anything beyond it
`INFERRED`. If the user's own numbers (from `metrics/`) disagree with the studies, the user's numbers win for
that account, once there are enough posts to tell. Under ten posts, there is no conclusion to draw.

## 4. Rhythm

- Fewer posts done well beat more posts done late. The default is 3 a week.
- Keep the weekdays steady for a month before judging a change.
- One language per post; a bilingual pair is two posts on the same day with the same claims.
- Leave one slot a week for something the news or the audience asked for.

## 5. What to stop

Every strategy review ends with one thing to stop doing and the reason. A plan that only adds is not a plan.

## 6. Check before you hand over

- Every planned entry has a pillar, an angle, a hook candidate and fact ids (or `NEEDS FACT`).
- `profile-wrap calendar validate` passes.
- The week fits the weekly ceiling and the gap.
- The user has seen the dry run of the build.
