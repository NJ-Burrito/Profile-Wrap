# Learnings

What your own posts have taught you, kept short on purpose (under 60 lines). Written by the weekly read
(`profile-wrap track prompt`), edited by you. Planning reads it as a hint: a learning never overrides a fact
in `research/facts.md` or a slot rule in `config/profile-wrap.json`.

Rules: a result from under 5 measured posts is a hypothesis. A learning is Confirmed only after two weeks
support it. Not measured is not zero.
