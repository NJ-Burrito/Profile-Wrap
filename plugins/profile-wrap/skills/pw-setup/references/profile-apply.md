# Applying a profile edit by hand

Profile-Wrap never edits LinkedIn. A profile rewrite (`profile-wrap profile`, prompt `profile-fix`) is a diff the
user applies in their own browser, by hand. This checklist is for that step. Nothing here is browser
automation, and no part of it may become one: it is a list of things to look at.

What follows was observed on one account in November 2026. LinkedIn changes its forms; read each line as
"check this", not as "LinkedIn always does this".

## Before you start

- Work from the sheet `profile-fix` wrote. Apply one section at a time and save it before the next.
- Lines marked `PLACEHOLDER` are not confirmed text. Rewrite them yourself first. A `PLACEHOLDER` line is not a
  fact, is never added to `research/facts.md`, and is never reused as the source of a post.
- A number in the new text must come from a verified fact. If it does not, leave the old line.

## While you apply

1. **Re-read the headline after every experience save.** LinkedIn's add-role and edit-experience forms can carry
   a headline field, and saving a role may set the headline to the role title. After each experience entry
   you save, go back to the top of the profile and read the headline. If it changed, put the intended headline
   back and check the field again before the next save.
2. **Escape discards the form.** Closing a form with Escape throws away what you typed without saving. Click
   Save, then check the page.
3. **Share and notify prompts.** After a save LinkedIn may offer to share the update or to notify your network.
   Choose not to notify unless you mean it. Do not accept suggested connections from the same screen.
4. **The skills section may have a cap.** If adding a skill is refused, remove one you no longer need first,
   and read every category tab, not only the first one, before deciding what is missing.
5. **A second-language profile** is edited from its own language switch. If you keep an Arabic and an English
   profile, apply both sides of a pair in the same sitting: a pair is one unit.

## After you apply

Keep a small log in the workspace (for example `profile/applied-<date>.md`), one row per section:

| Section | Applied | Verified on the live page |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | yes / no | what you read there, and the date |

List anything you did not do under `Not done`, with the reason. "Verified" means you read the saved page, not the
form you typed into. The headline row is checked last, after every experience entry.
