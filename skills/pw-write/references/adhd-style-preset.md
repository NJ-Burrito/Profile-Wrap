# Style preset: adhd_emoji

One preset, chosen in `voice.md` or `meta.style`. It is a look, not a rule: the caption rules, the
claim-bound numbers and the slop pass still apply. Original text.

## What it is for

Readers who skim. Short lines, one idea each, a visual rhythm, and an emoji that does a job. Use it
when the persona's audience reads on a phone in short bursts and the persona likes the look. Do not
force it on a persona whose voice is plain.

## Rules

1. **Topic hook in the first line**, under `gate.hook_target` (40) characters, led by one emoji that
   matches the topic. The gate warns when the first character is not an emoji.
2. **One idea per line.** Two short lines beat one long one.
3. **Emoji as bullets**, not as decoration: a leading emoji on most lines of a list, never inside a
   word, never more than one per line. Pick a small set and reuse it, so each emoji means one thing.
4. **White space between blocks.** One blank line between the hook, the points and the close.
5. **Few words per point.** Replace a sentence with a noun phrase when it keeps the meaning.
6. **Slides and images carry fewer words, bigger visuals**, one point per slide. Emoji belong in the
   caption only; on slides use vector icons from the brand kit, because emoji glyphs are not in the
   brand fonts and would fall back to another face.
7. **No emoji on a serious or sensitive topic** (loss, illness, layoffs).

## Example (fictional persona, no figures)

```text
📊 One page replaced the weekly report

🧭 The team opened it every Monday
🗑️ The old one sat unread
✂️ I kept the chart, dropped the table

❓ Which report did your team stop reading?
```

## Arabic

Emoji work the same in Arabic. Place the emoji at the start of the line (right side), keep one idea per
line, and check the result for mixed direction marks. See `arabic-hygiene.md`.
