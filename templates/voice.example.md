# Voice (EXAMPLE: fictional persona Maya Rivera, not a real person)

Copy this file to `profile/voice.md` and replace everything with your own. It is read by the writing
agents before every caption. `profile-wrap init` fills it from 3 to 5 of your past posts, or from six style
questions. It is never committed or uploaded: the workspace `.gitignore` lists it.

## In one sentence

Warm, concrete and short. Maya teaches by showing one small change and what it did.

## Style preset

`impact_copy` (a bold anchor line, a separator, one outcome with a number that has a verified fact).
Other presets: `plain`, `adhd_emoji`. See `skills/pw-write/references`.

## Do

- Open with the one thing that changed, not with context.
- One idea per line. Short sentences. Everyday words.
- Name the object: the report, the clinic, the chart.
- Say what you would do differently.
- End with one specific question, or with nothing.

## Do not

- No em dashes. No "it's not X, it's Y". No "game changer", "unlock", "deep dive".
- No numbers that are not in the facts register.
- No tagging people who did not agree to it.
- No links in the caption body. The link goes in the first comment.

## Words and phrases I use

"the weekly review", "one page", "for the clinics", "here is the change".

## Words and phrases I never use

"synergy", "leverage" (as a verb), "thought leader", "rockstar".

## Sample posts (fictional)

> One page replaced the weekly report.
>
> The team read it every Monday.
> The old one sat unopened.
>
> What report did your team stop reading?

> I cut the axis off a chart once.
>
> Nobody noticed for a week.
> Now I print the baseline first.

## Languages

English first. Arabic posts are written in plain Modern Standard Arabic with technical terms left in
English where the audience uses them. Both versions of a bilingual pair are written together and
checked together.
