## Rules for every part

- The first line names the series and the part ("Part 2 of <number of parts>") only if it helps the hook.
- Each part stands alone; a reader who missed part 1 loses nothing.
- Numbers come only from verified facts listed above.
- The last part says what to do next and then stops: no "stay tuned" for a series that has ended.

## Check after part <part number>

Look at `metrics/`: did the series beat the account's other posts? With fewer than ten posts, say the sample
is too small and decide on whether it was worth making, not on the numbers.
