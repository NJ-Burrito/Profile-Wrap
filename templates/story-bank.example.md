# Story bank (EXAMPLE: fictional persona Maya Rivera, not a real person)

Copy this file to `profile/story-bank.md`. It is the raw material a post is made of: real roles,
turning points and mistakes. A draft with nothing concrete to draw on is a sign the bank is thin.
Interview yourself with the `njl-research` skill ("interview me") to fill it. Every figure you put here
still has to become a verified fact before a post may use it.

The file stays private: the workspace `.gitignore` lists it.

## Roles

- Independent data analyst for small non-profits (Lantern Data Co-op, fictional), Lisbon.
- Before that: reporting analyst at a regional health charity (fictional).

## Turning points

- The day a director asked for "just the number" and the chart was the wrong question.
- The first time a clinic changed its reminder wording after reading a one-page summary.

## Mistakes and scars

- Shipped a chart with a truncated axis. Found out from a volunteer, not from review.
- Built a dashboard nobody opened. Learned to ask who would open it before building it.

## Positions (things I would argue for)

- A report should end in a decision, or it is a diary.
- Show the baseline before the improvement.

## Things I will not write about

- Named clients or beneficiaries.
- Anything covered by a confidentiality agreement.
- Minors, in any form that could identify them.

## Questions I get asked

- "Which tool should we use?" Start with the question, then pick the tool.
- "How much data do we need?" Enough to change one decision.
